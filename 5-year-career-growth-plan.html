Five Promises

What this plan commits to delivering
  • Close critical skill gaps in Year 1 to build a competitive foundation
  • Deliver measurable, documented impact on a high-visibility project by Year 2
  • Expand from execution to portfolio and people leadership by Year 3
  • Achieve recognized external presence through thought leadership by Year 4
  • Secure Director or VP role by end of Year 5

💰 Comp Milestone — End of Year 1

50th Percentile, Mid-Level PM

Confirm compensation is at or above the 50th percentile for mid-level PM in your target geography and sector. Use Levels.fyi, Glassdoor, and LinkedIn Salary. If a gap exists, request a specific meeting with market data in hand — do not wait for a review cycle.

Annual Review Checkpoint — Year 1

Complete within 30 days of year-end. Written outputs required.

  • Skills gap audit re-scored — every gap documented with current status and evidence
  • Five KPIs scored with supporting data, not self-assessment
  • Certification completed and documented in portfolio
  • Mentor feedback collected: written summary of mentor's readiness assessment
  • Compensation confirmed at 50th percentile or gap documented with action plan
  • Learning plan drafted for Year 2 with specific objectives tied to visibility goals

💰 Comp Milestone — End of Year 2

65th–75th Percentile or Senior PM Promotion

Reach the 65th–75th percentile or complete a promotion to Senior PM with a corresponding adjustment. Request a specific meeting titled "Discussion: Role and Compensation Alignment." Bring data. Frame as retention and alignment, not a complaint.

Annual Review Checkpoint — Year 2

Complete within 30 days of year-end. Written outputs required.

  • Impact portfolio reviewed with mentor and executive sponsor — feedback documented verbatim
  • External visibility assessed — professional profiles updated, external community joined
  • Promotion or scope expansion case evaluated — status documented with reason if blocked
  • Compensation confirmed at 65th–75th percentile or corrective action documented
  • Year 3 leadership targets set — specific portfolio and people leadership goals defined
  • External PM peer relationship established and first benchmarking conversation completed

💰 Comp Milestone — End of Year 3

75th–80th Percentile, Senior or Principal PM

If executing a strategic lateral move, use the transition to negotiate at or above this band. A move is the single most effective mechanism for a step-change compensation increase. Your new employer is pricing future value — your ask should reflect the Director-track scope you are stepping into.

Annual Review Checkpoint — Year 3

Complete within 30 days of year-end. Written outputs required.

  • Portfolio scope documented — list all programs, teams touched, and interdependencies owned
  • People leadership evidence compiled — development plans, outcomes, mentee feedback
  • 360-degree feedback collected from direct reports, peers, and senior stakeholders
  • Organizational path assessment completed with written conclusion and decision documented
  • External network assessed — 3–5 meaningful new relationships confirmed
  • Compensation confirmed at 75th–80th percentile or strategic move plan initiated
  • Year 4 thought leadership channel identified and first content or speaking pitch planned

💰 Comp Milestone — End of Year 4

Director Comp Floor

Reach the floor of the Director pay band in your target sector and geography. If you are already at a company with a Director role, this is your internal promotion target. If moving, this is your minimum ask in any offer negotiation. Use Levels.fyi and three direct recruiter data points.

Annual Review Checkpoint — Year 4

Complete within 30 days of year-end. Written outputs required.

  • Thought leadership: at least 5 pieces published, 1 conference talk delivered or accepted
  • External role confirmed and active (advisory board, committee, or non-profit board)
  • Target company relationships: at least 3 warm connections at Director-track orgs
  • Director JD gap analysis completed — final skill gaps documented
  • Personal narrative updated — 3-minute verbal and one-page written versions ready
  • Compensation at Director floor or offer-ready positioning confirmed

🏆 End Goal — Year 5

Director or VP of Program Management — Accepted Offer or Confirmed Promotion

The plan succeeds when you have a written offer letter or promotion confirmation at the Director or VP level, with compensation at or above the Director/VP market median for your sector and geography. Initiate your next 5-year plan within 90 days of starting in the role.

Annual Review Checkpoint — Year 5

Complete within 30 days of year-end. Written outputs required.

  • Target role defined and documented with full specificity (title, scope, comp band, company type)
  • Director/VP offer accepted or internal promotion confirmed in writing
  • Compensation at or above Director/VP market median for target geography and sector
  • 30-60-90 day plan active in the new role
  • Next 5-year growth plan initiated within 90 days of start date
  • Year 5 plan retrospective completed with mentor — lessons documented for future use
